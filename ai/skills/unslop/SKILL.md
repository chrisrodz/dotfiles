# Unslop

Edit text to remove AI patterns and add human voice.

## Process

1. Scan for the patterns below.
2. Rewrite. Preserve meaning and match the intended tone.
3. Add soul using the guidance below.
4. Ask, "What makes this obviously AI generated?" Fix the remaining tells.

## Add soul

- Have opinions. React to facts instead of neutrally listing pros and cons.
- Vary rhythm. Mix short sentences with longer ones.
- Acknowledge complexity. Specific tension beats generic praise.
- Use "I" when it fits.
- Let some mess in. Perfect structure looks machine-made.
- Be specific. Name the concrete concern or mechanism.

## Patterns to remove

### Content

1. Puffery such as "pivotal moment", "testament to", or "evolving landscape". State what happened.
2. Name-dropping media outlets without context. Pick one and say what it reported.
3. Superficial `-ing` phrases such as "highlighting" or "showcasing". Delete or support them.
4. Promotional language such as "vibrant", "breathtaking", or "groundbreaking". Use neutral descriptions.
5. Vague attribution such as "experts believe". Name the source or delete it.
6. Formulaic challenge-and-triumph framing. Replace it with specific facts.

### Language

7. Stock AI vocabulary: additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape as an abstraction, pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant.
8. Fancy replacements for "is" such as "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", or "features".
9. "Not just X, but Y." State the point directly.
10. Forced groups of three. Use the natural number of items.
11. Synonym cycling. Pick one term and repeat it.
12. False ranges where the endpoints do not form a meaningful scale.

### Style

13. Em dashes. End the sentence or use a comma.
14. Colons used as generic sentence connectors.
15. Boldface on every proper noun or acronym.
16. Inline-header lists that restate their labels.
17. Title Case headings. Use sentence case.
18. Decorative emoji.
19. Curly quotation marks. Use straight quotes.

### Communication artifacts

20. Chatbot phrases such as "I hope this helps", "Of course", or "Certainly".
21. Cutoff disclaimers. Find the source or remove the claim.
22. Sycophantic openings. Respond directly.

### Filler

23. Filler phrases. "In order to" becomes "To". "Due to the fact that" becomes "Because".
24. Excessive hedging. Keep only the uncertainty the evidence requires.
25. Generic conclusions. State the next action or concrete result.

### Jargon

26. Abstract metaphor nouns such as substrate, wedge, vector, locus, nexus, primitive, surface, bedrock, scaffolding, modality, paradigm, gold-plating, ratchet, evacuate, endgame, north star, and flywheel. Name the real mechanism.

### Plain speech

27. Say what something does, not how it feels. Use a mechanism, instruction, or number.
28. Split dense sentences. One idea per sentence.
29. Prefer active voice. Name the actor when it matters.
30. Cut adverbs or choose a stronger verb.
31. Prefer the plain word: use, help, many, and if.
